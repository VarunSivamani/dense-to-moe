Assignment method

Dense-to-MoE conversion

The decoder uses causal self-attention and a feed-forward network in each block. The trained dense feed-forward weights are copied into each expert at conversion.

For every token, the router selects two experts. Selection uses router logits plus a non-gradient expert bias. Output weights come from the unbiased router softmax.

Bias-only load adjustment favors underused experts. The language-model objective remains next-token cross-entropy; no auxiliary balancing term is added.

Assignment pipeline

From text to routed experts

Stages and measurements from the project workflow.

01 · DATA

Stream and tokenize

Hugging Face text is tokenized and written as compact uint16 IDs for memory-mapped sampling. The training log records 200M tokens; it does not identify the dataset revision.

02 · DENSE

Train the baseline

12-layer causal GPT · 704 width · 11 heads · 2,000 optimizer steps. Next-token cross-entropy falls from 11.02 to 4.37.

03 · UPCYCLE

Copy dense MLPs

Each block’s trained feed-forward network initializes all eight experts. A top-2 router is added at conversion.

04 · CONTINUE

Train the MoE

Another 2,000 steps with BF16 and the Triton backend. Bias-only expert balancing adds no auxiliary loss.
